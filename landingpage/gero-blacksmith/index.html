about me01

Gero Blacksmith is an artist and music producer working in electronic music. The name suggests a forge: sounds get heated, hit, bent and cooled into something new.

Most of the work happens in a personal studio, where tracks are written, tested and reshaped. Some end up as mixes, some as releases, some as live jams recorded in one take.

about my music02

  • idm

  • drum and bass

Broken rhythms and dense detail sit next to long, quiet textures. A track can go from a 170 bpm rush to a slow wash of ambient in a few minutes.

Breaks are chopped and rebuilt until they stop sounding like the originals. Bass is heavy but kept clean. Silence and space count as much as noise.

philosophy03

Make it by hand. Keep the mistakes that sound good. Finish the track, then start another.

Machines are instruments, not shortcuts. Dawless sessions mean every sound has a knob, a cable and a decision behind it.

Music is for listening closely and for moving to. It does not need to be one or the other.
